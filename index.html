    About

    People come before products.

    We help organizations perform better through technology. We advise, we integrate and we support, and we start with the people who will rely on the result every day.

    Our consulting philosophy

    Technology performs when people can use it well.

    Today's organizations deal with constant change, higher expectations from customers and stakeholders, and smaller budgets to meet them.

    In our experience the deciding factor is the team. A system only delivers value once the people around it understand it, trust it and are able to run it.

    Our approach

    Grow skills, strengthen teams, prove the outcome.

    Durable improvement takes time and starts with people. We support organizations at any stage, whether they are drafting their first IT plan or integrating a large estate of systems.

    Alongside the technical work, we address how people adopt change, so that individuals, teams and the wider organization can make full use of what is built.

    • Honesty
    • Integrity
    • Experience
    • Client trust

    Solutions

    Systems integration, end to end.

    We design, implement and support complex technology projects, so that infrastructure, business applications and data work together as one environment. Open a card to see what each includes.

    • Infrastructure and networks

      Servers, storage, networking and telecom equipment, selected, installed and configured to match how you work.

      You get
      • A reviewed and correctly sized architecture
      • Equipment installed, configured and documented
      • A tested move from the old setup to the new

    • Business applications

      Industry-specific software, implemented and adapted to your processes, with your team trained to use it.

      You get
      • Requirements agreed with the people who use the system
      • Configuration and customization
      • Training and a clear hand-over

    • Systems and data integration

      Links between your applications, databases and partner systems, so information moves without being retyped.

      You get
      • A map of how data flows today
      • Interfaces and APIs built and tested
      • Monitoring that shows when a connection fails

    • Cloud and hybrid environments

      Workloads placed where they run best: on your own premises, in the cloud or across both.

      You get
      • An assessment of what should move and what should stay
      • A migration plan with fallbacks
      • Cost and performance tracking after the move

    • Security and resilience

      Protection, backup and recovery designed in from the start instead of added afterwards.

      You get
      • Access and identity controls
      • Backup and recovery that is tested regularly
      • A plan for handling incidents

    • Managed services and support

      Monitoring, maintenance and a service desk, so that systems keep running well after go-live.

      You get
      • Proactive monitoring
      • Agreed response times
      • Regular reviews with ideas for improvement
